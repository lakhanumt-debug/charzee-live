Our Journey

From a Bold Idea to India's Trusted EV Charging Partner

A quick look at how Charzee has grown, milestone by milestone, on its mission to power a smarter, cleaner mobility future.

2019

Charzee Is Founded

Started with a single mission — build smart, reliable, and affordable EV charging infrastructure for India.

2020

First AC & DC Chargers Launched

Rolled out our first generation of smart AC and DC fast chargers for homes and commercial spaces.

2021

OCPP Platform Goes Live

Launched our OCPP-enabled Charging Management Software for real-time monitoring, billing and remote control.

2022

Solar-Integrated Charging

Expanded into solar-powered EV charging stations and solar power plants for a truly sustainable ecosystem.

2023

Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)

Introduced BESS solutions for backup power, grid stabilization and peak-load shaving across sites.

2024–Today

Scaling Across India

Now powering 100+ projects across 25+ cities, with a full range of EV, solar and BESS products and a growing nationwide network.
