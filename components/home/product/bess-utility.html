Key Features

Utility Series — full technical specification

Utility-scale containerized storage system delivering 5015kWh rated capacity.

Standard 20ft high-cube container format (6058 x 2438 x 2896mm).

LFP prismatic cells (3.2V/314Ah) in 1P416S12P configuration, 4,992 total cells.

Battery rack configuration of 8 modules per rack, 12 racks per system.

Rack nominal voltage of 1331.2V with a total capacity of 3768Ah.

Operating voltage range of 1164.8V–1497.6V for grid-scale integration.

Typical charge/discharge duration of 2–4 hours at 0.25C–0.5C max C-rate.

90% typical depth of discharge with 4514kWh usable energy.

Round-trip efficiency (RTE) of 85%.

Advanced liquid cooling (50% ethylene glycol + 50% water) with active thermal management.

Auxiliary power consumption of 50kVA/45kW for cooling and control systems.

Three-layered BMS architecture: Pack BMU + Rack BMS + Master BMS.

Full protection: OV/UV/OC/OT with heat, smoke, and gas detection sensors.

Automatic aerosol-based fire suppression with an optional water-based backup system.

SCADA-based smart control with Modbus TCP/IP connectivity.

UPS backup ensures uninterrupted safety and control during grid disruptions.

Fire-resistant container construction with optional C5-H surface coating.

Total system weight of approximately 45±1.5 tons.

Operating ambient temperature of -10°C to 55°C, altitude up to 3000m (with derating).

Built for utility operators and independent power producers requiring grid-scale renewable storage.
