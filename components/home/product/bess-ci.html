Key Features

C&I Series — full technical specification

Rack-based commercial and industrial storage system across 3 configurations.

Rated voltage options: 512V, 563.2V, and 665.6V depending on configuration.

Rated capacity of 100Ah or 314Ah using LFP prismatic cells (3.2V per cell).

Rated energy spanning 51.20kWh, 176.84kWh, and 66.56kWh across models.

Charge-discharge rate of 0.5C with 80% depth of discharge.

Max continuous charge and discharge current up to 157A (mid-capacity model).

Operating voltage range as wide as 448.8V–759.2V, depending on configuration.

Rack configuration of 10 to 13 packs per system (165:1P to 16S1P pack structure).

Forced air cooling with dedicated fans on each battery pack for thermal stability.

Cell-level BMS monitoring with SOH (State of Health) reporting and degradation tracking.

Safety logic executes an automatic emergency shutdown on fault detection.

Full protection: over-temperature, overcharge, overdischarge, short-circuit.

Communication via Modbus TCP/IP, RS485, and CAN for BEMS/SCADA integration.

IP54-rated enclosure for outdoor commercial deployment.

Single rack configuration per cabinet, expandable per site requirement.

Backed by a 5-year warranty (0.5C charge/discharge, 80% DoD at 25°C).

Designed for commercial and industrial facilities aiming to reduce peak demand charges.
