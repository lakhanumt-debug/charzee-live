Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Answers to common questions about solar power plant types, sizing, installation and monitoring.

On-Grid plants (around 70% of our deployments) export/import power with the utility grid. Hybrid plants (around 15%) combine solar, battery storage and grid supply. Off-Grid plants (around 15%) operate independently using solar and battery storage only.

Battery storage is optional for On-Grid plants but recommended for Hybrid and required for Off-Grid plants to ensure continuous power.

Capacity is determined after reviewing available rooftop or land area, daily energy consumption and future load growth.
